Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about the Kumarpur industrial plots, pricing, and infrastructure.

Where is the project located exactly?

The project is located in Kumarpur, West Bengal. It offers strategic connectivity to major transportation highways, enabling easy movement of raw materials and heavy vehicles.

What is the minimum plot size available for booking?

Plots can be booked starting from 1 Bigha. We offer flexible boundary options, vacant land, or completely filled land ready for immediate factory or warehouse construction.

What infrastructure is available in the industrial complex?

The complex is equipped with a 50 feet wide reinforced main road built for heavy containers, dispute-free clear title zoning, and optional security boundary walls for demarcated plots.

What are the payment terms and installment options?

You can book a plot by making a 35% advance payment. The remaining balance can be settled over a flexible installment period of 6 months.
