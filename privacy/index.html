Privacy Policy

Learn how we handle and protect your information across our website and the Kumarpur Land mobile application.

Last Updated: August 10, 2026

Adhikshita Plotters Private Limited ("we", "our", or "us") operates the website https://adhikshita.in and the Kumarpur Land mobile application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

1. Information Collection and Use

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Service to you.

Types of Data Collected:

  • Personal Data: While using our Service, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you ("Personal Data"). Personally identifiable information may include, but is not limited to:
    • Full Name
    • Phone Number (including WhatsApp number)
    • Interest/Plot selections and requirements
  • Usage Data: We may also collect information on how the Service is accessed and used. This Usage Data may include information such as your computer's Internet Protocol address (IP address), browser type, browser version, the pages of our Service that you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages, and other diagnostic data.

2. How Data is Collected

We collect data directly from you when you interact with our forms:

  • Online Inquiry Form: You voluntarily submit your Name, Phone Number, Plot Category, and additional requirements. This information is processed locally on your device to trigger a structured email inquiry.
  • Plot Booking Form: You voluntarily provide your Name, Phone, and required Plot size. Upon clicking submission, this data is formatted and redirected via the WhatsApp API to our sales contact (+91 7980560455) to initiate booking support.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies (like Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics) to track the activity on our Service and hold certain information. Cookies are files with a small amount of data which may include an anonymous unique identifier.

You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, if you do not accept cookies, you may not be able to use some portions of our Service. We respect Google Consent Mode preferences.

4. Use of Data

We use the collected data for various purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our Service.
  • To contact you in response to your plot inquiries or bookings.
  • To gather analysis or valuable information so that we can improve our Service.
  • To monitor the usage of our Service.

5. Transfer and Security of Data

Your information, including Personal Data, is processed securely. We take all steps reasonably necessary to ensure that your data is treated securely and in accordance with this Privacy Policy. All web traffic within our app and website is encrypted using HTTPS. Your information is never sold or shared with external third-party advertisers.

6. Data Deletion Request

We respect your privacy rights and provide a simple way to request the deletion of your personal data (such as Name and Phone Number) that you submitted through our booking or inquiry forms.

You can request deletion by clicking the link below, which will pre-fill a request to be sent directly to our sales contact via WhatsApp:

Request Data Deletion on WhatsApp
